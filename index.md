# Privacy Policy for ZEH Archer

**Last updated: September 15, 2026**

ZEH Archer ("the Game") is developed and published by **ZEH Games**.

ZEH Games respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use ZEH Archer.

## Advertising

ZEH Archer uses Google AdMob to provide rewarded and interstitial advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, device and network information, and advertising interactions in accordance with their own privacy practices.

Where required, ZEH Archer uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

## In-App Purchases

ZEH Archer uses Google Play Billing for in-app purchases, including an option to remove advertisements.

Payments and transaction processing are handled by Google Play. ZEH Games does not directly receive or store users' payment card information.

## Game Progress and Settings

ZEH Archer stores certain game information locally on the user's device. This may include:

- Game progress and unlocked levels
- High scores
- Selected and owned cosmetic items
- Music and sound settings
- Virtual currencies and energy
- Advertisement timing information
- Remove-ads entitlement status

This information is stored locally on the user's device.

ZEH Games does not currently operate its own backend server for transmitting or storing this local gameplay data.

## Analytics and Crash Reporting

ZEH Archer does not currently use Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Unity Analytics, or another developer-operated analytics or crash-reporting service.

## User Accounts

ZEH Archer does not currently require users to create an account.

The Game does not currently use Google Play Games authentication.

## Children and Age-Appropriate Use

ZEH Archer is intended for the audience declared for the Game on Google Play.

Advertising and consent mechanisms are configured in accordance with applicable Google Play policies, Google advertising requirements, and applicable privacy laws.

## Third-Party Services

ZEH Archer uses third-party services provided by Google, including:

- Google AdMob
- Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
- Google Play Billing

These third-party services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

Google's Privacy Policy is available at:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Data Security

ZEH Games takes reasonable measures to protect information associated with the Game. Gameplay data managed directly by ZEH Archer is currently stored locally on the user's device rather than on a ZEH Games backend server.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if ZEH Archer's features, services, advertising systems, or data practices change.

The latest version of this Privacy Policy will be published on this page.
